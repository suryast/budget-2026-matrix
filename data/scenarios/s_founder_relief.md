This branch is narrower than a full repeal but wider than the original founder-relief placeholder. ABC reported on 18 Jun 2026 that the government intends to expand the 50 percent active asset discount from a 2 million dollar turnover ceiling to 10 million dollars, and to consult on a startup carve-out where qualifying new, innovative businesses choose between a 50 percent discount and an inflation discount. Founders, early-stage investors and employees granted shares as remuneration are in scope, but the final eligibility tests are not yet settled. Use hedged language: it is a real policy signal, not a completed statutory safe harbour.
